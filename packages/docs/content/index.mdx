Regolith Rail is a sandbox for freight dispatch on shuttle rail lines. You write a
policy that decides what each train loads and unloads at every stop, run it on a
scenario, and compare it with other policies over many random seeds.

## Where to start

- [Getting started](/docs/getting-started) walks through a first run, an edit and a
  batch comparison.
- [Operations research on the line](/docs/book) is a book that teaches the ideas behind
  better policies, from modelling and simulation to inventory control, with a scenario
  to run in every chapter and every result checked.
- [Library and saved runs](/docs/library) explains choosing what to run, your own
  copies, saved runs, share links, and import and export.
- [Language and sandbox rules](/docs/language) covers the Lua that policies are
  written in, and what the sandbox allows.
- [The ops pipeline](/docs/ops) introduces the building blocks that let most policies
  be a few lines long.

## Why the baseline struggles

Each starter scenario shows one way that simple stock balancing goes wrong:

- [Half capacity](/docs/book/modelling#case-study-half-capacity): one train, two stations, and a
  shortage that never closes.
- [Dead stock](/docs/failure-modes/dead-stock): a relay station holds what the dome needs.
- [Double dispatch](/docs/book/base-stock#case-study-double-dispatch): two trains answer the same shortage.
- [Ping-pong](/docs/failure-modes/ping-pong): goods travel back and forth for nothing.
- [Disruption recovery](/docs/failure-modes/disruption-recovery): no safety stock to absorb a
  supply or demand shock.
