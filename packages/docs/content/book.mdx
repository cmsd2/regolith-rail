Operations research is the study of how to make good decisions in systems that run by
rules: how much to stock, when to order, where to send a vehicle, and how to tell whether
a change made things better. This book introduces it by doing. Every chapter has a
scenario you can open in the workbench, a policy to run and change, and exercises whose
answers are checked.

The main text uses the general language of the field: stations, reviews, lead times and
demand. A side note in each chapter connects the topic to rail lines in the game.

## How results are checked

Each formula and number in a chapter is checked. Mathematics is verified with the
Maxima computer algebra system or Python, and statements about the simulator by running
it. Open **Check** beside a result to see the working.
