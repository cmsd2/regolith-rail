Regolith Rail is a fan-made sandbox for experimenting with freight dispatch on shuttle
rail lines, inspired by the railways of Surviving Mars: Relaunched.

## Not affiliated

Regolith Rail is not affiliated with or endorsed by Paradox Interactive or Haemimont
Games. It uses no game assets: every picture, name and number here was made for this
project.

## Licence

The source code and documentation are released under the
[Apache License, Version 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The source
is on [GitHub](https://github.com/cmsd2/regolith-rail).

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Policies, scenarios and results are not sent anywhere.
Drafts and saved work stay in your browser's storage, and share links carry their
contents in the part of the address after `#`, which browsers do not send to the site.
