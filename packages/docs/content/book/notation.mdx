Symbols keep one meaning throughout the book, with two exceptions noted in the tables: $L$, which is
the average number present in Little's law and the lead time elsewhere, and $D$, which is a constant
demand rate in chapter 4 and a random demand from chapter 5 on. A symbol used only inside one derivation,
such as the stock at a named station, is defined where it appears and left out here.

Rates are per day unless a chapter says otherwise, and times are in hours or days as stated. Costs are in
whole cost units.
